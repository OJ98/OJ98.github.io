# About

I am a PhD student in the Computer Science Department at NC State University, advised by [Dr. Munindar Singh](https://www.csc2.ncsu.edu/faculty/mpsingh/). My research explores how Multiagent Protocols and Large Language Models can be used to model and analyze complex human reasoning, particularly in the context of argumentation and debate.

## Research Interests

My current work focuses on:
- **LLM-driven MultiAgent Protocols:** Modeling social interactions and norms in AI systems.
- **Computational Argumentation:** Extracting and evaluating argumentative structures in natural language.
- **NLP for Social Science:** Using language models to understand human social behavior.

## Background

Prior to starting my PhD, I completed my Masters in Computer Science at NC State and worked as a developer at Credit Suisse. My passion for argumentation stems from my background in **International British Parliamentary debating**, where I have competed and adjudicated at several international tournaments.
